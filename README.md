The toolkit groups your match preparation and match review into clear sections. Here is what each part is for.

A Striker profile is where you record how you like to play a particular character setup. The toolkit lets you save several profiles side by side so you can compare them without starting over each time. Build and store loadouts for the weapons you use most. Each loadout entry can hold the attachments and configuration you prefer, plus a short note on when to use it. This section gives you a structured space to practise aiming and controlling weapon recoil by hand. Plan your team before a match begins. Note who is covering which role, which loadouts the group is bringing, and what the fallback plan is if the opening approach does not work. Squad plans can be saved and reused, which is useful when you play regularly with the same group of people.

### 📊 Match Tracking

Every match you play can be logged with the details that matter to you: the map, the outcome, the loadout you used, and a short summary of what happened. Keeping a running record means you can look back at a month of matches and see real patterns instead of relying on memory.

### 📈 Combat Analytics

The analytics section turns your logged matches into simple, readable summaries. The goal is to answer practical questions, such as which setup deserves more time and which one is quietly holding you back.
